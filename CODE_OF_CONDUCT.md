# Code of Conduct

Everyone participating in Hospiq issues, pull requests, reviews, and project discussions is expected to help maintain a respectful and useful environment.

## Expectations

- Discuss ideas and technical evidence without attacking people.
- Welcome contributors with different backgrounds, abilities, and experience levels.
- Give actionable feedback, accept corrections, and respect reasonable boundaries.
- Protect privacy: do not publish personal information, patient data, or credentials.
- Keep discussions relevant and avoid spam or misleading claims.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexual harassment, doxxing, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Moderation and reporting

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed. Responses should consider severity, context, and repetition. Affected participants may ask for clarification or review through a private channel agreed with the maintainer.

Contact the [maintainer](https://github.com/adarshprakasan) to arrange a private reporting channel. If no private channel is available, open an issue requesting one without naming affected people or publishing sensitive details. This policy does not promise a monitored emergency contact or a fixed response time.
